## Table tennis

I have played table tennis for more than fifteen years. I have remained active in table tennis clubs throughout this time, where I have met many like-minded friends. I play Japanese penhold.

## Games

I particularly enjoy *Arknights*, especially its roguelike mode, as well as *Civilization VI*, *Hollow Knight*, and *Hollow Knight: Silksong*.

## Reading

I enjoy philosophical and psychologically rich novels, including Thomas Mann's *The Magic Mountain* and the works of Fyodor Dostoevsky. In a different vein, I also enjoy Central European prose and novellas, particularly works by Sándor Márai and Stefan Zweig.

## Hiking and fitness

Hiking and working out help me clear my head, recharge, and enjoy time away from the desk.
